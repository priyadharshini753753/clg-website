About Departments

Explore our departments, academic strengths, and the institution behind MI CLUB.

B.Sc Computer Science

The Computer Science (CS) is committed to nurturing future-ready professionals through strong foundations in programming, computer applications, and emerging technologies. With a strong focus on technical skills, problem solving, communication, and practical learning , the department encourages students to participate in seminars, workshops, coding challenges, and innovative activities. Through continuous learning and innovation, the department strives to develop confident, competent, and socially responsible professionals.

B.C.A (Bachelor of Computer Application)

The Computer Applications (BCA) focuses on developing students with strong knowledge of computer applications, programming, software development, and information technology. Students are encouraged to develop logical thinking, technical skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities through practical learning and academic activities. The department prepares students to meet the growing demands of the IT industry and higher education.

B.Sc Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence)

The Artificial Intelligence (AI) introduces students to modern technologies that are transforming the digital world. The department focuses on areas such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, data-driven technologies, and intelligent systems. Students are encouraged to explore innovative ideas, develop practical solutions, and gain knowledge about emerging technologies through projects, workshops, and hands-on learning.

B.Sc Computer Science (Data Science and Analytics)

The Data Science And Analytics (DSA) focuses on helping students understand data, analytics, statistics, and modern computational techniques. Students develop skills in data analysis, visualization, programming, statistical methods, and data-driven decision making. The department promotes practical learning and encourages students to use data and technology to solve real-world problems.
